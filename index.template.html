About me

I am a PhD researcher in robotics at University College London (UCL), working with Chengxu Zhou and the Humanoid Robotics Lab. I study how humanoid robots can learn to move robustly and expressively in the real world.

My research brings together reinforcement learning, imitation learning, generative motion models and whole-body control. I am particularly interested in connecting learned motion with real-time sensory input, from versatile locomotion to dance generated from streaming music.

Previously, I conducted doctoral research at the University of Leeds and worked on model-based bipedal control at Tencent Robotics X. I received my MSc from Imperial College London and my BEng from the University of Sheffield.

Research interests Humanoid robotics · learning for control · whole-body motion · sim-to-real transfer
